How to Play

  • 🔲 Tap a tile to reveal a letter
  • 💣 Avoid bombs — one click ends the game
  • 🟨 Yellow tiles hint nearby word letters
  • ❌ Cross means letter is not in the word
  • 🟫 Brown tile = correct letter found
  • ⏳ Complete the word before total moves finish
  • 🎯 Find all 4 letters to win
